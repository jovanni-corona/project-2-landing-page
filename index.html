My Story

My family is from Mexico, and tacos al pastor have been part of my life for as long as I can remember. Whenever I get the chance to order tacos, these are always the ones I choose.

My mom was the one who made them for us, usually on the weekends. The whole house would fill with the delicious smell of tacos al pastor, and that smell meant the family was about to come together. Those meals are some of my favorite memories.

If you've never had tacos al pastor before, this recipe is a great place to start.

Instructions

This recipe takes three simple steps and is ready in about 30 minutes.

  1. Make the Al Pastor Sauce

    In a blender, add all the sauce ingredients and blend until smooth. Set aside.

  2. Cook the Al Pastor Pork

    Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, then add the onion and garlic. Add the ground pork and break it into small pieces with a wooden spoon as it cooks, until it is no longer pink. Stir in the al pastor sauce until well combined. Lower the heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until most of the liquid has evaporated. Taste and add more salt if needed.

  3. Serve

    Serve the meat hot on warm tortillas, topped with freshly chopped cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
